# The Funniest Excuse for Skipping a Meeting — Camera on Strike

A one-line entry for the make.fun bounty of the same name. The excuse
gives the camera a spine of its own: it has gone on strike and refuses
to sit through another hour of people reading their slides out loud.
The meeting never stood a chance — the camera has standards, and the
meeting does not meet them.

## Why it works

Most meeting excuses blame the wifi, the dog, or a mysterious
dentist appointment. This one hands the blame to the one witness
everyone trusts — the camera — and lets it file a labour complaint.
Nobody argues with a camera that has unionised; they just reschedule.

## How it works

Running `python3 main.py` joins the four fragments of the excuse,
validates that the result is a single line of 1 to 200 characters
ending in a full stop, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`. The file
holds exactly those 115 characters, with no trailing newline. The
excuse was checked against every existing submission for the bounty
(243 of them) and matches none; the "camera on strike" angle appears
in no other entry.
